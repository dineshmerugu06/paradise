    Our story

    A legend since 1953

    The world-famous Paradise brand began in a small canteen and café in a cinema theatre called Paradise in Secunderabad in 1953. A small 100-seater restaurant started serving typical Hyderabadi cuisine — biryani and more — from the early sixties.

    Even today, Paradise Biryani uses the same traditional recipe that made it legendary more than six decades ago. The authentic Hyderabadi Dum Biryani is meticulously prepared in layers, flavoured with saffron, and served with raita and mirchi-ka-salan. We use only Halal meat in all our preparations.
